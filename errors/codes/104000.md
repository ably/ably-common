code: 104000
identifier: streamed_message_finalize_failed
title: Streamed message could not be finalized
summary: A streamed message could not be completed on the channel. One of its appends was not published, and the follow-up attempt to repair it by re-sending the accumulated content in a single update also failed.
---

## What you should do

The partial message that had been streamed is left on the channel, and the write that raised this error has stopped. Produce the reply again: under a durable-execution framework such as Temporal, the engine retries the failed step for you; otherwise start the reply again from your application, which in a chat client is a regenerate.

If retries keep failing, inspect the error's `cause` for the underlying failure rather than assuming a retry will fix it.

## Why it happens

A streamed message is delivered as a publish followed by a series of appends. Appends are sent without waiting for each to be acknowledged, so a failed append is noticed only when the stream ends or its writes are flushed. At that point the SDK repairs the message by re-sending its full accumulated content in a single update. This error is raised when that repair also fails. The originating failure is preserved as the error's cause.

## What you'll see

The error is reported with code 104000 and status 500. The message takes the form `unable to repair stream; ...`. An event writer reports it from `flush()` and `close()`, and from the next write after it. A pipe reports it as the `cause` of a pipe error (code 104008).
